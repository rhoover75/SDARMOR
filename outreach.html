SD-ARMOR / Outreach

Outreach & workforce pipeline

A "grow our own" strategy running from middle school through graduate research, feeding directly into the center's three pillars.

CybHER

The nation's largest residential girls-only cybersecurity camp, co-founded by A. Podhradsky and sustained through NSA/NSF GenCyber grants and corporate partnerships. Runs alongside the Governor's Cyber Academy as a direct K–12 pipeline into the center's cybersecurity workforce mission.

125+ students / year

K–12 & STEM pipeline

Embedded in South Dakota's NSF E‑CORE RII network (Black Hills State / CAMSE, PI Sayler), operationalizing a K–12 STEM pipeline and broadening participation across rural and tribal communities statewide.

Student research pathways

  • REU / NRT — embedded student researchers rotate across pillar labs statewide.
  • Cross-pillar seed grants — $10–25K awards inviting affiliate faculty from any SD Board of Regents institution.
  • Cyber-Informed Engineering degree — planned joint SD Mines / DSU pathway, pending Board of Regents approval.

USTART Innovation Exchange

Biannual partner-sourced problem cycles led by D. Engebretson, where center faculty, postdocs, and students produce IP-ready artifacts routed directly into SBIR/STTR pathways.
