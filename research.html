Why three pillars

The vulnerability runs through all three

Compromised logistics networks, seized communications infrastructure, and a lost domestic production base are three parts of the same failure — the reason SD-ARMOR treats materials, cybersecurity, and provenance as one problem rather than three separate research programs.

The infrastructure exposure

Programmable logic controllers (PLCs) govern physical processes across U.S. energy, water, food, and defense-manufacturing infrastructure. Over 90% of industrial control system cyber incidents involve PLCs or SCADA devices, and the global PLC supply chain is concentrated in ecosystems subject to foreign state oversight.

South Dakota as proving ground

A September 2025 CISA Infrastructure Impact Assessment concluded that South Dakota's critical infrastructure — communications, energy, food and agriculture, transportation, water — is a documented target. SD-ARMOR's research agenda is set directly by that operational threat picture.
